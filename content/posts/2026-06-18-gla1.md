It's great to see the increasing interest in Glasgow about Rust, and so we are kicking-off some meetups there. 

To start with we're having some coffee/pub meetups (on the same day, Thu 18th June):
* ☕️ A daytime coffee event at 13:00 (this event)
* 🍻 An evening pub event at 18:00 (see [other Meetup Event](https://www.meetup.com/rust-and-friends/events/315093500/))

Please feel free to come to both 🙂 This a "vote with your feet" situation: come along to find out about Rust and also to let us know what you'd like to see and/or help with.

Look out later in the year for some presentation sessions, which we're also currently organising.
